# Overview

Welcome to the **Multimodal Data Analysis Session (MDAS): Cancer & Cardiac Research**. This hands-on event introduces practical workflows for working with different types of biomedical data using KaleCancer and KaleCardiac, two domain-focused libraries built on [PyKale](https://github.com/pykale/pykale).

## What you'll explore

- **Cancer:** Combine clinical information and pathology patch features to explore time-to-event prediction with a synthetic cohort.
- **Cardiac:** Explore ECG leads and chest X-ray/ECG fusion through two synthetic-data demonstration workflows.
- **Your own research:** Discuss which data modalities, outcomes and practical constraints matter in your project.

## Who this is for

AI/ML researchers, data scientists, research software engineers, clinicians, clinical researchers and PhD students working with, or planning to work with, cancer or cardiac data. Some Python experience and a laptop are recommended.

## Start here

1. **Learn about the session.** Explore [about MDAS](about.md), the [draft programme and learning objectives](agenda.md).
2. **Prepare your environment.** Review the [prerequisites](prerequisites.md) and [setup instructions](quick-start.md) before the session.
3. **Choose a tutorial.** Select either [KaleCancer](../tutorials/kalecancer/README.md) or [KaleCardiac](../tutorials/kalecardiac/README.md) to explore practical biomedical data analysis workflows. See the [tutorials overview](../tutorials/README.md).
4. **Record your observations.** Use the [participation guide](participation.md) to record your observations, questions, and findings during the hands-on activities.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The synthetic examples are demonstrations of software workflows, not evidence of clinical performance.
