# What is MDAS? About the session

## Purpose

Explore how multimodal machine learning can combine biomedical data types, and gain confidence adapting example workflows to research questions in cancer and cardiac applications.

The planned Multimodal Data Analysis Session (MDAS) introduces the **early, intermediate and late fusion** ideas at a high level before participants explore practical examples. A clinician can focus on what data are used, what is predicted, how results are interpreted, and where limitations arise; a technical participant can also inspect configuration and code.

## Proposed format

- Brief introduction to multimodal fusion and the libraries.
- Guided synthetic-data demonstration of loading, encoding, prediction and evaluation.
- Supported time for participants to apply ideas to their own suitable data or a provided problem.

> [!NOTE]
> The session format is provisional and subject to confirmation.
