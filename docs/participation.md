# Participation

## Suggested activity loop

1. **Choose a research question.** What is the outcome and what data are available?
2. **Run a synthetic example.** Follow the relevant tutorial, keeping its default configuration first.
3. **Inspect outputs.** Record the predicted endpoint, metric and any interpretation artifacts.
4. **Discuss limitations.** What would change with real data, missing modalities, class imbalance or censoring?
5. **Ask for support.** Use the facilitator team to identify a safe next experiment.

## Bring your own data?

> [!WARNING]
> The planning draft proposes allowing participants to work with their own data or organizer-provided alternatives. Availability of suitable datasets and support is not yet confirmed. Any clinical data must be used only with appropriate permissions and secure infrastructure.

## Questions to discuss

- Which modalities are available for each subject?
- Is the endpoint a classification, regression or time-to-event outcome?
- How can subject-level separation prevent data leakage?
- What does a synthetic run prove, and what does it not prove?
