# Tutorials Overview

Choose one of two research tracks. Each starts with synthetic data so you can explore a pipeline without patient datasets.

## Cancer

Multimodal cancer research workflows, including clinical and pathology data, model training and evaluation. Explore intermediate fusion and a Cox time-to-event model.

[Start the Cancer tutorial](kalecancer/getting-started.md).

## Cardiac

Cardiac analysis workflows, including CardioVAE for chest X-ray + ECG representation learning and LS-EMVAE for lead-specific ECG representations and fine-tuning.

[Start the Cardiac tutorial](kalecardiac/getting-started.md).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Synthetic demonstration results show how the software pipeline works. They are not clinically validated results or evidence of clinical predictive performance.
